# ENewbold ADR Template

# ADR-NNN: Decision title

## Status

`Proposed`, `Accepted`, `Superseded`, or `Deprecated`.

## Context

State the architecture problem, relevant constraints, and why a durable decision is required.

## Decision

State the selected architecture clearly and identify the important boundaries or invariants it establishes.

## Alternatives Considered

For each realistic alternative, summarize material advantages, costs, security implications, operational implications, and migration impact when relevant.

## Consequences

Record positive and negative consequences, new constraints, coupling, maintenance burden, and future flexibility.

## Security Implications

Describe authentication, authorization, tenant, credential, secret, privacy, and data-exposure implications when applicable.

## Operational Implications

Describe deployment, monitoring, support, reliability, and ownership impacts when applicable.

## Migration and Compatibility

Describe contract changes, schema/state migration, rollout sequencing, and backward-compatibility implications when applicable.

## Related Decisions and Documents

Link related ADRs and architecture documents.
